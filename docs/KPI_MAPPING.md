# KPI mapping — form criterion → OrangeHRM KPI

Generated from `shared/criteria.json` (version final-2026-08). This is what
`npm run sync-kpis` creates. Every KPI is rated **1–5** and sits on the job
title named in each heading.

The KPI title is the criterion with its explanatory clause trimmed, because
OrangeHRM shows KPI titles in narrow table columns. The full wording still
appears on the form and in the summary PDF.

## Where each number comes from

| KPI | Filled from |
|---|---|
| Role criteria | The **lead’s** scorecard (Form 1–7). If the lead marked it N/A it is left blank in OrangeHRM rather than guessed. |
| Peer review — pooled average | Average of every peer review, once 2+ are in. |
| Upward review — pooled average | Average of every upward review, once 3+ are in. Leads only. |
| Review **final rating** | The blended score: lead 60%, peer 25%, upward 15%, re-normalised over whichever groups counted. |

Peer and upward numbers deliberately go in as their own KPIs rather than
being averaged into the role criteria — so nobody reading the OrangeHRM record
mistakes a peer average for the lead’s judgement of a specific criterion.
